    Teplo Association

    The name of our association, Teplo (тепло), means "heat" in Ukrainian.


    Since 2014, members of the working group, which later became the core of the Teplo association, have actively participated in activities in support of Ukraine: they provided humanitarian aid, organized cultural events such as a Reuters photography exhibition and an anti-war conference (Arles, France, 2014).

    Since spring 2022, Teplo has adapted its support for Ukraine:
    in response to the challenges threatening the lives of thousands of people and civilization itself, it officially registered as a non-profit organization under the French Law of 1901. It thus joined the pan-European movement to aid Ukrainian refugees.
    In addition to providing volunteer translators, lawyers, and psychologists, Teplo has begun developing new formats of effective and accessible psychological support for all victims, while also helping to formalize the claims of victims of PTS     (Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder).

    In this new reality, the number of people suffering heavy losses and in need of assistance, particularly psychological support, is increasing exponentially, and existing support systems are becoming increasingly inaccessible due to the sheer number of refugees.

    The association's initial work focused primarily on establishing activities to help Ukrainian refugee children: the most vulnerable victims of the military conflict. This means providing psychotherapeutic support to their family members, both those already in Europe and those remaining in Ukraine. Without this psychological support, the children's eventual recovery of their inner equilibrium is impossible.

    ​

    While initially the focus of our efforts was Ukraine, recently our intention to cooperate with specialists from the Lebanese Association of Psychotherapy and with this group of Israeli psychotherapists, who participated in the 2022 practice exchange conference, has acquired particular importance.
