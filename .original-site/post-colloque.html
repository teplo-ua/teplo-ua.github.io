    International scientific and practical symposium "Therapeutic Gardens" in Meze

    • teplofr
    • December 6, 2024
    • 2-minute read

    "Therapeutic gardens as an innovative and effective method for the psychotherapy of people suffering from traumatic syndromes".ASSOCIATION LOI 1901 TEPLO, in collaboration with the creator of the concept Roger S. Ulrich, the French Federation Jardin Nature Santé organized in Mèze on November 29, 2024 "Therapeutic gardens as an innovative and effective method for the psychotherapy of people suffering from traumatic syndromes".



    Therapeutic gardens, as a tool of evidence-based medicine, have immense untapped potential worldwide, particularly relevant today in the face of large waves of people displaced by armed conflict, many of whom require psychotherapeutic support.

    The participants of this symposium – the pioneer of the method, Roger Ulrich, Ph.D., honorary professor at Texas A&M University and Chalmares University, Sweden –

    as well as his disciples and colleagues, who apply this approach in the therapy of various population groups in Western Europe, with the participation of European specialists from other disciplines (Birgit de Boissezon, former director of the Department of Sustainable Management of Natural Resources at the European Commission, Denmark, Svetlana Fernandez, holder of a master's degree in project management, founder of the TEPLO association, Salvetat-sur-Agout, France, Laure Bentze, ecologist, specialist in horticultural therapy and psychotherapist, member of the federation, director of the company Terr'Happy, France, Isabelle Boucq, clinical psychologist, founder and former president of the federation, France, Dorthe Djernis, clinical psychologist, PhD, Denmark, Tanya Ekelin, master of science, psychomotor therapist, Oasis-Rehab, clinic for refugees and victims of war suffering from traumatic disorders, Denmark),

    and with the active participation of Ukrainian psychotherapists – specialists working with combatants and civilians who have suffered from Russian aggression

    exchanged their acquired experiences and methodological reflections, and also agreed to create a European consortium to participate in the Life and Horizon funding programs.
